About Us

Who We Are

"Quality is not an act, IT IS A HABIT"

Arcuzo Decor Pvt. Ltd. is a well-known fenestration company with more than 25 years of acquired experience in window design and fabrication. Operating under the umbrella brand of AlupPure, we continuously innovate and develop new product and profile systems.

We have a strong base in Punjab, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Chandigarh, Uttarakhand and Delhi — creating solutions in windows, doors and facades for residential and commercial projects alike.
